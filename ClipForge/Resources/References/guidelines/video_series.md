SERIAL VIDEO
“101 in 101” 
Professors give us the 101 of their field or an aspect of their field in ONLY 101 seconds.


This social video friendly concept challenges professors to distill the introductory magic of a 101 course into an elevator pitch aimed at students to encourage them to study their field with a time limit of 101 seconds or less.
Like an intro class, this should be a brief overview of the material but with one or two big takeaways about what the researcher/professor finds fascinating and exciting about their field. Each talk should be formatted similarly and neatly fit into a minute and a half.
Production:
Interview time: 30-60 minutes. Recorded in the PA/Comms studio on a gray background, please don’t wear gray!

Published playlist here.
Sample Format:
Introduction: "I’m (NAME) a professor of (X) at (UC Berkeley/Department/Lab/Center) and I've been challenged to explain the 101 of XXXXX in 101 seconds, here we go!"
First 30 seconds: What is XXXXX? What parts of our lives does it touch? The more relatable and visual this list, the better.  List a few areas of study and their importance today. Is there a short list of 3 examples that many people would be familiar with?
Second 30 seconds: What drew you to this field? What is it about XXXXXX that you love? Was there an "aha" moment that made you realize this was your calling?
Third 30 seconds: Where is the XXXX field going right now? What are the big problems the field is focused on? Why is this research critical at this moment and why should students pursue it at Berkeley?


Intended Audience:
Students/Prospective Students. General audience.
Can be used by each department or school for their website and outreach for prospective students.
Runtime: 
TRT 2 minutes.
